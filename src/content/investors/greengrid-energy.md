## The opportunity

GreenGrid develops, builds and operates solar generation and storage for
industrial consumers in markets where grid supply is expensive or unreliable.
Revenue comes from long-term power purchase agreements rather than merchant sale.

## How the capital is used

Proceeds fund the development pipeline through to financial close, at which point
project debt takes over the construction cost.

## Reporting

Generation and availability data is published monthly; financial reporting follows
the quarterly cycle.
