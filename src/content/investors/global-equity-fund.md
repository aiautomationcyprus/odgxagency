## The opportunity

Global Equity Fund allocates growth capital across the sectors we already operate
in — energy, logistics, healthcare and industrial technology — with the benefit of
operating diligence rather than desk research.

## How the capital is used

Investment is staged against operational milestones rather than calendar dates.
Each tranche is released when the underlying business hits the performance markers
agreed at the outset.

## Reporting

Holders receive quarterly performance reporting, an annual audited statement, and
notice of any material change to the portfolio within five working days.
