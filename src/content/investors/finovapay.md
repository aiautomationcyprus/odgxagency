## The opportunity

FinovaPay consolidates banking, payments and reconciliation for businesses
operating across several currencies and jurisdictions — the segment large banks
serve poorly and local providers cannot serve at all.

## How the capital is used

Capital funds licensing in three additional markets and the compliance function
those licenses require.

## Reporting

Regulated entity reporting is filed quarterly and published alongside our own.
