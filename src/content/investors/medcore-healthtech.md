## The opportunity

Medcore puts diagnostic imaging within reach of clinics that cannot support a
resident radiologist, combining remote reporting with AI triage to prioritize the
urgent cases.

## How the capital is used

Capital funds regulatory approval in two further markets and the clinical
validation studies those approvals require.

## Reporting

Clinical outcome data is published annually; financial reporting is quarterly.
