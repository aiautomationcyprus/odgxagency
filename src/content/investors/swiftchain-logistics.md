## The opportunity

Swiftchain reconciles carrier, forwarder and customer data into a single shipment
record, then predicts delays early enough for customers to route around them.

## How the capital is used

Capital funds carrier integrations and the data science team building the delay
model.

## Reporting

Operating metrics are reported quarterly alongside the financial statement.
