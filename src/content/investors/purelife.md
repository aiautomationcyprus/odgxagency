## The opportunity

Purelife builds distributed generation alongside water treatment in regions where
neither is reliably available, funded through a blend of commercial and
concessional capital.

## How the capital is used

Commercial capital funds the generation assets; concessional capital funds the
water infrastructure that the generation makes viable.

## Reporting

Impact and financial reporting are published together in a single annual report.
