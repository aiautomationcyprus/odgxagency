## Service Overview

We plan, finance and operate renewable generation for industrial groups that need
clean power at scale — and the reporting to prove it. Our teams work from site
selection through to the operational data that lands in your annual report.

## What we deliver

### Generation portfolio development

Site assessment, permitting, financing and construction management for solar,
wind and hybrid assets across multiple jurisdictions.

### Grid integration and storage

Connection strategy, storage sizing and dispatch modelling, so generation lines
up with demand instead of fighting it.

### Power purchase and offtake

Structuring long-term agreements that hedge exposure and give the business
predictable energy cost over the life of the asset.

### Operations and asset management

Continuous monitoring, predictive maintenance and performance optimization across
the portfolio.

### Regulatory and ESG reporting

Emissions accounting and disclosure aligned to the frameworks your markets
require.
