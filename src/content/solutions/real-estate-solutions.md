## Service Overview

Property is usually the second largest line on the balance sheet and the least
actively managed. We treat a real estate portfolio as an operating asset: planned,
developed and run against targets.

## What we deliver

### Portfolio strategy

A view of every owned and leased asset, its cost, its utilization, and what it
should become.

### Development and capital projects

Site acquisition, design management and construction delivery for new build and
major refurbishment.

### Transactions and leasing

Acquisition, disposal and lease negotiation handled with the portfolio strategy in
mind.

### Facilities and operations

Day-to-day building management measured on cost, energy and occupant experience.

### Sustainability retrofit

Decarbonizing existing stock, where most of the emissions actually sit.
