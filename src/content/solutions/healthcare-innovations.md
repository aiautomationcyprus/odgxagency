## Service Overview

Healthcare technology fails when it is designed away from the ward. We build
clinical and operational systems alongside the people who use them, under the
privacy and safety constraints the sector demands.

## What we deliver

### Clinical platforms

Record systems, scheduling and care pathways designed around clinical workflow
rather than administrative convenience.

### Patient data and interoperability

Standards-based integration between systems that were never meant to talk to each
other, with consent and access properly modelled.

### Connected devices and diagnostics

Device telemetry, imaging pipelines and diagnostic tooling integrated into the
clinical record.

### Privacy, safety and compliance

Architectures built for regulated data from the first design review, not
retrofitted before launch.

### Operational analytics

Capacity, throughput and outcome measurement that helps services plan.
