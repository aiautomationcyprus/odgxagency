## Service Overview

We help groups deploy capital deliberately: where it earns the most, at a risk the
board has actually agreed to, with reporting that survives an audit. Our teams sit
between the operating businesses and the capital markets that fund them.

## What we deliver

### Capital allocation and portfolio strategy

A single view of where capital is committed across sectors, and a framework for
deciding where the next tranche goes.

### Treasury and risk management

Liquidity planning, currency and rate hedging, and counterparty exposure managed
across every entity in the group.

### Transaction advisory

Diligence, valuation and integration planning for acquisitions, disposals and
joint ventures.

### Investor relations and reporting

Disclosure, earnings material and the underlying data pipeline that produces it.

### Compliance and controls

Financial controls designed for a group operating under several regulators at
once.

## Value we create

### Better returns on committed capital

Decisions made from a portfolio view rather than a divisional one.

### Lower financing cost

A credit story that lenders and rating agencies can follow.

### Fewer surprises

Exposure that is measured before it becomes a problem.
