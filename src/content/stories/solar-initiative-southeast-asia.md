## The challenge

Manufacturing clients across Southeast Asia faced rising grid tariffs and
increasingly frequent outages, with no realistic path to on-site generation at the
scale they needed.

## What we did

We developed a distributed portfolio of rooftop and ground-mount solar across four
countries, structured under long-term power purchase agreements so clients carried
no capital cost. Storage was sized against each site's outage profile rather than a
single standard.

## The outcome

120MW of installed capacity, an average 28% reduction in energy cost for
participating sites, and a measurable improvement in production continuity at the
sites where outages had been most disruptive.
