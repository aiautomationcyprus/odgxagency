## The challenge

Most of a property portfolio's emissions sit in buildings that already exist.
Certification programs tend to reward new construction, which is where the least
of the problem lives.

## What we did

We surveyed the entire portfolio, ranked assets by emissions per square metre
rather than by age, and retrofitted in that order — fabric and controls first,
plant replacement only where it was genuinely due.

## The outcome

Certification achieved across the majority of the portfolio, with energy intensity
down sharply and the works funded largely from the operating savings they produced.
