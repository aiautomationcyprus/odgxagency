Sustainability sits outside the business in most organizations, which is exactly
why it loses every argument it enters.

## Put it on the operating scorecard

Emissions, waste and energy intensity belong next to output and margin, reviewed
by the same people at the same meeting. Separate reporting lines produce separate
priorities.

## Make the trade-offs explicit

Some decisions genuinely cost more in the short term. Saying so, with the figure
attached, is more persuasive than pretending every green choice pays for itself
immediately.

## Design it in

Retrofitting sustainability into a finished design is the expensive path. The
cheap window is the same one where you choose materials, sites and suppliers.

## Hold suppliers to the same line

Most of a manufacturer's footprint sits upstream. Procurement standards move more
carbon than any on-site initiative.

## Report the misses

Credibility comes from publishing the targets you did not hit, and what changed
as a result.
