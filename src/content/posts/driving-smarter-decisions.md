The most common failure in a data program is not a broken pipeline. It is two
people in a meeting with different numbers for the same thing.

## Definitions are the product

Before the warehouse, before the dashboards, agree what a customer is, when
revenue is recognized, and which sites count as active. Written down, owned by
someone, versioned like code.

## Make the source obvious

Every figure on a report should be traceable to the system it came from and the
transformation applied. Trust follows traceability.

## Serve decisions, not curiosity

A dashboard nobody acts on is a cost. Start from the decision that needs making
and its cadence, then build the smallest thing that informs it.

## Automate the recurring, not the exploratory

Recurring reporting should require no human effort. One-off analyzis should
require no ceremony. Confusing the two produces slow answers and brittle
pipelines.

## Retire aggressively

Reports accumulate. An annual cull of anything unopened for a quarter keeps the
estate honest.
