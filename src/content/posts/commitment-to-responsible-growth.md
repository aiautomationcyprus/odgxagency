A multinational can either apply the local minimum in every market or hold one
standard everywhere. We have chosen the second, and it is occasionally expensive.

## One safety standard

Site safety requirements do not vary by jurisdiction. Where local rules are
lighter than ours, ours apply.

## One approach to pay and conditions

Wages are benchmarked to a living standard in the local market rather than to the
legal floor.

## Local supply where it holds up

We buy locally where suppliers meet our standards, and we invest in bringing
suppliers up to them rather than importing around the problem.

## Transparent tax

We report what we pay and where. Structures that exist only to move profit are not
worth the reputational cost.

## Independent verification

Internal assurance is not enough on its own. Our environmental and labor
standards are externally audited, and those findings are published.
