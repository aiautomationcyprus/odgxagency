Every transformation program is a workforce program wearing different
clothes. The software is the easy half.

## Skills follow the work, not the plan

Training built before the new process exists teaches the wrong things. Build the
process, run it with a small group, then teach what that group actually needed.

## Middle management is the constraint

Front-line teams adopt quickly when their supervisors do. Programs that brief
executives and end users while skipping the layer between them stall in exactly
that layer.

## Make the old way harder

If the previous process still works, most people will keep using it. Retiring the
old path is usually the single most effective adoption lever available.

## Hire for adaptability

Specific tool knowledge dates within a couple of years. The ability to learn a new
system without needing to be walked through it does not.

## Keep the feedback loop short

Weekly contact with the people doing the work catches design mistakes while they
are still cheap to fix.
